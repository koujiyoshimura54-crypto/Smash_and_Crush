## Phase 1.2 — fixed departure-Strength offline reward

Old formula: equipment-derived seconds rate × 5%, capped at 3,600 seconds. New formula:
`departureStrength * (0.04 * (min(max(elapsed,0),86400)/3600))`.
The bounded fraction is evaluated before multiplication to avoid an unnecessary intermediate overflow. Fractional Strength is preserved; there is no floor or minimum grant.

The server snapshots departure Strength once before yielding save/retry work. The existing StudioStartOverride preservation continues to use the underlying persisted Strength, so a debugging join override cannot become a durable earning basis. Login or claim-time equipment/Strength never recalculates the reward. The existing direct grant path applies no additional Dumbbell/Aura/Item/Pet/Treadmill/VIP/rebirth/Potion effects. StrengthReference and all three purchase-pack formulas are unchanged.

State envelope Version2 distinguishes `Presence.Basis="DepartureStrengthV1"`, `Strength`, `At` and `State`. Legacy `Rate` means gain per second and is accepted only as legacy data, never interpreted as Strength. Without a confirmed new-format Offline snapshot, the first migrated login receives **zero new offline reward**; a subsequent confirmed departure establishes the new basis. This also avoids treating an expired Online heartbeat as proof of an offline interval.

Every new owner/login replaces the unclaimed free reward with that interval's reward. Existing pending paid intents retain their exact original amounts, reward/product/receipt bindings and duplicate fences. Envelope migration does not reprice paid transactions. Confirmed old paid amounts, including fractions, remain payable.
